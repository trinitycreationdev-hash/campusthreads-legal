Delete Your Beaver Account & Data

You can request deletion of your Beaver account and associated personal data at any time.

Account & Data Deletion

Beaver allows users to delete their account and associated personal data through the application.

Beaver is built around Spaces and Stacks. Your account may contain profile information, participation in Spaces, messages, Stacks, preferences, and other information associated with your use of the service.

Important: Once your account deletion request has been completed, deleted data may not be recoverable. Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

We recommend using the in-app deletion option whenever possible because it provides the most direct way to request deletion of your account.

How to Delete Your Account in Beaver

  1. Open the Beaver app.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information presented to you.
  6. Confirm your account deletion request.
Deleting the Beaver application from your device does not delete your Beaver account.

What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on the information associated with your account, account deletion may remove personal information and account-associated content such as:

Account Information

Information associated with your Beaver account, such as your account identifier, display name, username, and other profile information.

Profile Information

Profile image, profile details, and other information that you have added to your profile.

Messages & Content

Messages, posts, images, links, and other content associated with your account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Stacks

Stacks that you created, including Stack names, descriptions, and associated personal preferences or saved information.

Space Activity

Account-associated participation and activity within Spaces, where applicable.

Preferences

Personal settings, preferences, and information used to personalize your Beaver experience.

Notifications

Notification-related information associated with your account, including notification tokens where applicable.

Account Connections

Account-associated relationships, memberships, follows, or other connections within Beaver, where applicable.

What Happens to Your Stacks?

Stacks are a core part of Beaver and are designed to help users organize information around their personal interests.

When you delete your account, personal Stack information associated with your account may be deleted or anonymized as part of the account deletion process, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

This may include Stack names, descriptions, preferences, and account-associated information.

What Happens to Spaces?

Spaces are community areas where users communicate and share information.

Deleting your account does not necessarily mean that an entire Space will be deleted, particularly when other members depend on that Space.

Where your account has participated in a shared Space, some community information may need to remain available to other members in an appropriate form.

We may remove, anonymize, or otherwise disassociate account-specific information where appropriate and technically feasible.

Content Shared With Other Users

Beaver is a community platform, which means that some content may have been shared with other users through Spaces.

Depending on how the content was shared and how the service operates, some community content may need to remain available as part of the shared history of a Space.

Where appropriate, information may be removed, anonymized, or disassociated from the deleted account.

Data Retention

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days.

Some information may remain temporarily in secure backups or systems until those systems are updated or overwritten.

Certain information may also be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

When retained data is no longer required for these purposes, it will be deleted or appropriately handled in accordance with our applicable policies.

Information That May Be Retained

Account deletion does not necessarily require the immediate deletion of every piece of information from every system.

Information may be retained for a limited period when reasonably necessary for:

  • Compliance with applicable laws and legal obligations.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Prevention and investigation of abuse.
  • Resolving disputes or enforcing legal rights.
  • Maintaining system security and integrity.
  • Secure backup and disaster-recovery processes.

  • Account deletion may permanently remove information associated with your Beaver account.
  • Deleted account data may not be recoverable.
  • Shared community content may be treated differently from private account information.
  • Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.
  • Email deletion requests may require account ownership verification.
