Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Beaver, operated by Trinity Creation, is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for everyone who uses our platform.

We take the protection of children and the prevention of child sexual abuse and exploitation extremely seriously.

These Child Safety Standards explain our approach to preventing, detecting, reporting, and responding to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and other forms of harmful activity involving children on Beaver.

Zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation or abuse.

Beaver does not permit content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, or targets children.

Our Commitment

Beaver is designed to support communities and conversations around topics and interests. Because users can communicate and share content, safety is an important part of operating the platform.

We are committed to:

  • Prohibiting child sexual abuse and exploitation material.
  • Prohibiting sexual exploitation or grooming of children.
  • Taking reports involving child safety seriously.
  • Taking appropriate action against accounts that violate our safety rules.
  • Removing prohibited content when identified, subject to applicable law and our policies.
  • Cooperating with appropriate authorities and organizations when legally required or otherwise appropriate.
  • Continuously improving our safety and moderation processes.

Prohibited Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation Content

Beaver strictly prohibits any content that sexually exploits or abuses children.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexualized images or videos involving children.
  • Sexual content involving or targeting minors.
  • Requests for sexual images or material involving minors.
  • Sexual solicitation or exploitation of children.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual relationships with children.
  • Content that encourages, promotes, glorifies, or facilitates child sexual exploitation or abuse.
  • Attempts to buy, sell, exchange, distribute, or obtain child sexual abuse material.

Other Harmful Behavior Involving Children

We also prohibit behavior that places children at risk of harm.

G

Grooming

Attempts to build inappropriate relationships with minors for sexual or exploitative purposes are prohibited.

S

Sexual Solicitation

Users may not solicit, request, encourage, or exchange sexual content involving minors.

E

Exploitation

Using Beaver to exploit or facilitate the exploitation of children is strictly prohibited.

H

Harmful Contact

Behavior intended to place children in dangerous or exploitative situations is prohibited.

Zero-Tolerance Policy

We may take immediate action when we identify activity that involves child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, action may include:

  • Removing or restricting prohibited content.
  • Restricting or disabling accounts.
  • Restricting access to Spaces.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally permitted or required.
  • Reporting suspected illegal activity to appropriate authorities or relevant organizations when required or appropriate.

We do not permit Beaver to be used as a place to exploit, abuse, or sexually target children.

How to Report Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior on Beaver that you believe involves child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or another serious child-safety concern, please report it to us immediately.

In-App Reporting

Beaver may provide in-app reporting tools that allow users to report messages, accounts, or other content directly from the application.

If an in-app reporting feature is available, we encourage users to use it for content that violates Beaver's safety policies.

For urgent child-safety concerns, you may also contact us directly using the email address provided above.

Safety and Moderation

Beaver may use a combination of automated systems, user reports, moderation processes, and human review to identify content or behavior that may violate our policies.

Our safety systems may evolve as Beaver grows and as new risks are identified.

No moderation system can guarantee that every violation will be detected immediately. Users should report concerning content when they encounter it.

User Responsibilities

Everyone using Beaver has a role in maintaining a safe community.

Users must not:

  • Upload, request, distribute, or promote CSAM.
  • Sexualize or exploit children.
  • Groom or sexually solicit minors.
  • Encourage others to engage in child exploitation.
  • Use Beaver to arrange or facilitate harmful activity involving children.
  • Attempt to evade Beaver's safety or moderation systems.

Enforcement

When we determine that an account or piece of content violates our child-safety policies, we may take appropriate enforcement action.

Enforcement decisions may depend on the severity of the conduct, the circumstances, applicable law, available evidence, and whether there is a continuing risk to users.

Serious violations may result in permanent account termination without prior notice where permitted or required by law.

Cooperation With Authorities

Trinity Creation may cooperate with law enforcement, child protection authorities, and other appropriate organizations when required by law or when necessary to protect children and users.

Where legally required, we may preserve or disclose relevant information in response to valid legal requests or reporting obligations.

Important Safety Information

Beaver cannot guarantee that every person using the platform is who they claim to be.

Users should exercise caution when communicating with people they do not know personally.

Do not share personal information, passwords, financial information, precise location information, or other sensitive information with strangers.

If you believe a child is in immediate physical danger, contact your local emergency services or appropriate child-protection authorities immediately.

Updates to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time as Beaver evolves, new safety risks emerge, or legal and regulatory requirements change.

When we make significant changes, we may update the effective date shown at the beginning of this page.
