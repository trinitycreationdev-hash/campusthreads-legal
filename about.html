What is Beaver?

Beaver is a community platform designed to solve a simple problem: important information gets buried in conversations.

In large communities, thousands of messages can be posted every day. A useful internship opportunity, an important job notification, a new anime announcement, a gaming update, a fashion discovery, or a piece of useful information can easily disappear as new conversations continue.

Beaver is designed to make sure that the information that matters to you has a better chance of staying within your reach.

The Problem

Modern communities are full of information, but finding the information you actually care about can be difficult.

Imagine a Space with 100,000 members. Hundreds or thousands of messages could be exchanged every day.

Somewhere inside those conversations might be:

  • A new internship opportunity.
  • A job notification.
  • An important announcement.
  • A new anime release or update.
  • A gaming announcement.
  • A fashion discovery.
  • A useful educational resource.
  • A link or piece of information worth remembering.

But once the conversation moves forward, finding that information again can become difficult.

Beaver is built around the idea that useful information shouldn't simply disappear because the conversation moved on.

Two Simple Ideas: Spaces & Stacks

S

Spaces

Spaces are communities where people gather, communicate, share information, and discuss topics they care about.

A Space can be focused on almost anything — technology, internships, jobs, gaming, anime, fashion, education, hobbies, or other interests.

T

Stacks

Stacks are personal collections created by you to represent the information you want to keep track of.

You can create Stacks such as Internships, Jobs, Gaming, Anime Updates, Fashion, or anything else that matters to you.

Built Around What Matters to You

Everyone uses communities differently.

One person might care about internships and jobs. Another might care about gaming and technology. Someone else might want anime updates, fashion discoveries, travel information, or educational resources.

Your Stacks give Beaver a way to understand the categories of information you consider important.

Over time, your interactions with Spaces and content can help Beaver identify information that may be relevant to your interests.

The goal is simple:

You choose what matters. Beaver helps you find more of it.

Discover Communities

Beaver is not only about keeping information. It is also about discovering the communities where that information comes from.

Through Spaces and discovery features, you can find communities built around topics and interests that you care about.

Join conversations, meet people with similar interests, and discover information that may be useful to you.

What Makes Beaver Different?

Traditional community platforms are primarily designed around conversations.

Beaver is designed around both conversation and information.

Spaces provide the community.

Stacks provide the personal layer that helps you keep track of what matters to you.

Together, they create a system where conversations can become a source of useful, personalized information rather than something that simply disappears into an endless feed.

Our Vision

The internet contains an enormous amount of useful information. The challenge is not always finding information — it is finding the information that matters to you at the right time.

Beaver aims to build a better way for people to participate in communities while keeping the information they care about close at hand.

Communities for conversation. Stacks for what matters.
