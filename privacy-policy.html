1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Beaver, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Content that you create, upload, post, or share.
  • Information about Spaces and Stacks that you create or join.
  • Information associated with your interactions with community content.
  • Device and technical information required to operate and secure the service.
  • Information required to deliver push notifications when you enable notifications.

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for providing, maintaining, securing, and improving Beaver.

2. Account Information

When you create or use a Beaver account, we may process information associated with your account, such as:

  • Email address.
  • Authentication information.
  • Display name or username.
  • Profile photo, if you choose to provide one.
  • Other profile information that you voluntarily submit.

Authentication services may be provided through Firebase Authentication or other authentication infrastructure used by Beaver.

3. Content You Provide

Beaver allows users to participate in communities and organize information through Spaces and Stacks.

Depending on the features you use, we may process content such as:

  • Messages and posts.
  • Images or other media that you upload.
  • Links or URLs that you share.
  • Space names and descriptions.
  • Stack names and descriptions.
  • Profile information.
  • Reports, feedback, and support requests.

You are responsible for ensuring that the content you submit to Beaver does not violate applicable law or the rights of others.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Beaver, certain technical information may be processed automatically to operate and protect the service.

Depending on the configuration of the application and its underlying services, this may include:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Basic diagnostic information.
  • Crash and error information.
  • Network and connectivity information.
  • Identifiers required by third-party service providers.

We use this information primarily for security, troubleshooting, reliability, and improving the performance of Beaver.

5. Notifications

Beaver may use push notification technology to send you relevant notifications about your account, Spaces, Stacks, community activity, or other features of the service.

Push notifications may use a device or application messaging token provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

You can control notifications through the notification settings provided by your device and, where available, through Beaver's settings.

6. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your Beaver account.
  • To authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • To provide Spaces, Stacks, messaging, profiles, and other application features.
  • To organize and surface content according to features you choose to use.
  • To deliver notifications.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and other harmful activity.
  • To respond to feedback, support requests, and reports.
  • To diagnose errors and improve application performance.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

7. Public and Community Content

Beaver is a community-based application. Content that you intentionally publish within a Space or otherwise make available to other users may be visible to members of that Space or other users depending on the applicable feature and visibility settings.

You should therefore avoid posting sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, private contact information, or other information that you do not want other users to see.

Please remember that information shared with other users may be copied, saved, or shared by those users outside Beaver.
